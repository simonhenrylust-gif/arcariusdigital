FAQ · Soalan lazim

Frequently asked

Do you buy gold?

Yes — bring it in and we'll weigh and price it in front of you. You can also message us first for today's rate.

How is the price decided?

Gold is priced by weight and purity at the day's rate, so prices change daily. Ask us for today's price.

Can I reserve a piece?

Message us with a photo or description and we'll let you know if we can hold it for you.

Do you do custom orders?

Ask us — tell us what you have in mind and we'll check what's possible and how long it takes.
