How pickup works

Three steps, no chasing a chat thread

Every order lands at the right branch with a pickup time on it, so the kitchen knows exactly when to fire it.

1

Choose your branch

Mata-Mata, One Riverside, Jerudong or Seria. Your order goes straight to that kitchen.

2

Pick a pickup slot

Choose a time that suits you. Busy slots fill up and close, so the kitchen never takes on more than it can make on time.

3

Walk in, collect, go

You get a confirmation with your branch and time. Your pizza is made for that slot, not when you arrive.
