1. The offer

Arcarius Digital provides fixed-scope website packages at a flat, one-time price, plus an hourly redesign service for businesses that already have a website. There is no monthly fee and no subscription on any option. Four options are available:

  • Starter — RM899: a mobile-first website built for your business, plus your Google Business Profile fixed, included free.
  • Complete — RM1,499: everything in Starter, plus one manual process fixed to match how your business runs — either your WhatsApp booking turned into a simple online booking page, or a basic order page if you're an F&B/food business. One or the other, matched to your business type at the start of the project.
  • Complete Plus — RM2,989: everything in Complete, but both a booking page and an order page are built, plus a priority build slot.
  • Makeover — RM128/hour: a redesign of your existing website's look and feel, plus your Google Business Profile fixed, included free. A booking or order page can be added if needed. Scoped and quoted in estimated hours after we've seen your current site, before any work begins.

Exact inclusions per tier are as described on our website at the time you sign up.

2. Who this is for

This package is built around six business types: kedai runcit, bengkel, salon, tuition centre, F&B/food business, and grocery store. If your business is outside these categories, contact us on WhatsApp to discuss whether we can help.

3. Timeline

Before any work begins, we agree a fixed timeline with you directly over WhatsApp. Work proceeds against that agreed scope and timeline rather than as an open-ended engagement.

4. Payment

A 50% deposit is due before any paid work begins, with the remaining 50% due on delivery. For Makeover, the deposit is 50% of the estimated hours quoted upfront; if the job runs longer than that estimate, the extra hours are agreed and invoiced separately before we continue. Exact payment details are confirmed with you directly over WhatsApp. Once a deposit or payment has been made and work has started, that payment covers work already completed and is non-refundable, except where required by law.

5. The MSME Digital Grant

The Geran Digital PMKS Madani (MSME Digital Grant) may offset up to 50% of your package fee — whichever tier you choose — up to a maximum of RM5,000, for businesses that are SSM-registered (or registered with a local authority/PBT, or SKM for cooperatives), at least 60% Malaysian-owned, operating for at least 6 months, averaging at least RM50,000 in annual turnover, and not a previous recipient of this same grant. Figures per Bank Simpanan Nasional's (BSN) published terms as of this writing — grant terms can change between funding cycles, so eligibility should be confirmed with BSN or an appointed Digitalisation Partner at the time of application. Arcarius Digital can assist with preparing and submitting this application on your behalf, but approval is decided solely by the grant administrator and is not guaranteed by us.

6. Content and information you provide

You're responsible for providing accurate business information, images, and content for your website, or for approving any content we draft on your behalf before your site goes live. We build your website based on the information you give us — inaccurate or incomplete information may affect what we can deliver within the agreed timeline.

7. Ownership

Once your package is paid in full, the delivered website and its content are yours. We may reference completed builds as examples of our work, including using them as demo builds shown on our own website, unless you ask us not to.

8. What's not included

Each tier includes exactly what's listed in Section 1 for that tier — Starter has no booking or order page, Complete has one, Complete Plus has both. Any work outside the agreed scope for your chosen tier, such as an additional process fix beyond what your tier includes or ongoing changes after launch, is a separate conversation and a separate agreement.

9. Limitation of liability

We build your website to a professional standard based on the information and content you provide, but we can't guarantee specific business outcomes, such as a particular level of enquiries or sales. To the extent permitted by Malaysian law, Arcarius Digital's liability in connection with this package is limited to the amount paid for it.
