Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

1. Who we are

Arcarius Digital is a one-person Malaysian business run by Simon, building fixed-price websites for Malaysian businesses. This policy explains how we handle personal data collected through this website and through our WhatsApp and email conversations with prospective and existing clients, in line with Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA).

For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at arcariusdigital@gmail.com or via WhatsApp at +60 16-426 1472.

2. What personal data we collect

We only collect what we need to talk to you, quote your project, build your website, and, if you ask us to, help with your MSME Digital Grant application. This includes:

  • Your name
  • Your WhatsApp number / phone number
  • Your email address
  • Business details you share with us, such as your business name, business type or vertical, and SSM registration details if you provide them for a grant application
  • The content of your messages to us over WhatsApp or email, including any content, images, or business information you send us for your website build
  • Payment reference details when you pay for your website — for example, a bank transfer reference or receipt image. We do not collect or store your bank login, card number, or card security code; payments are made directly to our bank account and we never see or store your card or banking credentials

This website itself does not use cookies or tracking scripts, and it has no contact form — all contact happens directly through WhatsApp or email, where the above information is shared by you, directly, in that conversation. Our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information, such as IP address and browser type, for security and performance purposes; we do not use this for tracking or marketing.

3. Why we collect it

We use your personal data to:

  • Respond to your enquiry and understand what your business needs
  • Build and deliver your website and your booking or order page
  • Fix your Google Business Profile as part of your package
  • Prepare and confirm your invoice, and process payment confirmation
  • Help with your MSME Digital Grant application, if you ask us to
  • Provide support after your website is delivered
  • Keep records we're legally required to keep, such as for tax purposes

We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not sell it to anyone.

4. How we store and protect your data

As a one-person operation, your data is stored in a small number of places: our WhatsApp Business app, our business Gmail inbox, and, where relevant, project files and invoices kept on password-protected devices and accounts. We don't use third-party marketing tools, data brokers, or ad-tracking services. We don't share your data with any third party except where necessary to deliver your project — for example, sharing SSM and business details with an MDEC-listed grant administrator when you've asked us to help with a grant application, or sharing files with a hosting provider to publish your website.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as we need it: for the length of your project, for a reasonable period afterward in case you need support, and, for financial records such as invoices and payment references, for as long as Malaysian tax law requires businesses to retain such records (generally up to seven years). After that, we securely delete it.

6. Your rights under the PDPA

Under the Personal Data Protection Act 2010, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any personal data that is inaccurate or out of date
  • Withdraw your consent to us processing your data, where consent is the basis for processing
  • Request deletion of your personal data, subject to any legal or record-keeping obligation we must still meet

To exercise any of these rights, message us on WhatsApp at +60 16-426 1472 or email arcariusdigital@gmail.com with your request. We'll respond as soon as reasonably possible, and in any case within 21 days.

7. Third parties

We only share your data with third parties where it's necessary to deliver what you've asked for: an MDEC-listed grant administrator, only when you've asked us to help with a grant application; and our website hosting provider, to publish your site. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers.

8. Children

This website and our services are directed at business owners and are not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal data, we'll update this page and update the "last updated" date at the top.
