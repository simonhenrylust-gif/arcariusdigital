MSME Digital Grant

Currently closed — here's the real status

The Geran Digital PMKS Madani closed to new applications on 14 November 2025, with no reopening date confirmed yet by the Ministry of Finance. We'll update this the moment that changes. Worth knowing either way: a plain business website isn't currently listed among the grant's eligible categories (Digital Payment/e-POS is the one specifically highlighted), so eligibility for a website build isn't guaranteed even once applications reopen.

Questions

Frequently asked

Are these prices really the full price?

Yes. RM899, RM1,499, or RM2,989 depending on the tier you pick — flat, one-time. No monthly fee, no subscription, nothing hiding a "real" price behind it.

What's the difference between the packages?

Starter (RM899) is just the website plus a free Google Business Profile fix. Complete (RM1,499, most popular) adds one manual process fixed — your WhatsApp booking turned into a simple booking page, or a basic order page if you're an F&B/food business. Complete Plus (RM2,989) gets you both a booking page and an order page, plus a priority build slot. Makeover (RM128/hour) is for redesigning a website you already have, quoted in estimated hours after we've seen it.

Can I get a booking page and an order page?

Yes, with Complete Plus. Starter has neither, Complete gets you one matched to how your business runs, and Complete Plus gets you both.

How long does it take?

We agree a fixed timeline with you upfront, before any work starts — typically 2–5 business days depending on your request, no open-ended waiting and no vague "a few weeks" guessing.

My business isn't a kedai runcit, bengkel, salon, tuition centre, F&B/food business, grocery store, or retail store — can you still help?

Message us on WhatsApp and let's talk. Those seven are what we're built around, but reach out anyway.

What's the MSME grant, and do I qualify?

It's the Geran Digital PMKS Madani — but as of writing, it's closed to new applications (both 2025 intakes have ended, no reopening date confirmed), and a plain website isn't currently listed among its eligible categories anyway. See the grant section above, or just ask us on WhatsApp.

Why WhatsApp instead of a contact form?

Because that's where you already run your business and talk to your customers. A contact form is one more inbox you'll forget to check.

Do you update or fix websites you didn't build?

Yes — updating an existing website (yours or a previous developer's) is a separate paid job, quoted after a quick look at your site so we know what we're actually working with. No subscription, no monthly retainer — you only pay when you need something changed. Message us on WhatsApp with what you need updated.

Which hosting platform do you use for my site?

Reliable, professional hosting, handled entirely on our end — you don't need to think about servers or uptime. Ask us on WhatsApp if you want the specifics for your build.
